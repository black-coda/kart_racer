For our game:

A GPU commonly represents 3D surfaces using triangles.
A triangle has three vertices.
A collection of triangles forms a 3D mesh.


Compare the two projection types:
- Perspective: Objects appear smaller as they move away.
- Orthographic: Objects retain the same apparent size regardless of distance.
